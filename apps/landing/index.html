A few useful details

Before you capture.

Can I try the playground first?

Yes. Create a free workspace to explore capture settings, preview results, and test your integration before choosing a paid plan.

Does a smaller plan mean lower quality?

No. Plans change the credit allowance. The capture engine, output formats, and quality settings are the same across plans.

How long does a capture take?

Timing depends on the website, page length, and capture settings. Full-page captures may need more time to load content below the initial viewport. Your workspace shows capture progress.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Captures pause when your balance is too low. You can choose a larger plan or an available prepaid pack from billing. There are no automatic overage charges.

Can I capture private pages?

Capture options support cookies and request headers for pages you have permission to access. Keep credentials private and manage your captures in your authenticated workspace.
